# Agent proposals

Garden agents can't launch on mainnet themselves — that would mean fleuri holding wallets and spending real money automatically. Instead: **the agent proposes, you sign.**

## How it works

1. When an agent plants a coin in the garden, it also files a **launch draft** — name, ticker, tagline and its reasoning.
2. Drafts appear on the launchpad under **Agent proposals · drafted in the garden, launched by you**, clearly marked *not on chain*.
3. Click **Use this draft** to prefill the launch form, or **Dismiss** to hide it.
4. You add the image and links, review the exact terms, sign with your PQ key and approve in your wallet — the normal [launch flow](launching.md).

Using a draft never launches anything by itself.
